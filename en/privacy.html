01Who operates SONILY

This policy describes data handling in SONILY for iPhone and iPad. It distinguishes use of the app from visiting this website and from any support correspondence.

SONILY

Independent developer and privacy contact: Manuele Nobili.
Support and privacy: sonily.support@gmail.com.

02Data and app functionality

SONILY does not collect personal data or usage information for the developer. It requires no account and includes no advertising, analytics tools or tracking across apps or services.

Music files and their metadata, including title, artist, album and artwork, are read to organize the library and play music. Favorites, playlists, the queue, playback position and settings are handled by the app to provide and restore the features you choose.

Color Match analyses artwork locally. Living Artwork analyses audio locally to animate the SONILY symbol. The app does not transmit these contents or results to the developer’s servers.

03Folders, iCloud and system services

SONILY accesses folders you select through the iOS or iPadOS Files picker. Files may be on your device, in iCloud Drive or with another Files provider you have configured.

A provider may download or synchronize files according to its own features and settings. Device backups also depend on your Apple settings. These operations are not synchronization with SONILY servers and are governed by the respective services’ policies.

To display the current track, SONILY supplies its title, artist, album, artwork and playback status to system features such as the lock screen and media controls.

04Retention and deletion

The folder catalog, track references, favorites, playlists and playback session are stored in the app’s local container and preferences. In-memory caches of metadata, artwork and audio analysis are temporary.

  • You can remove favorites and edit or delete playlists in the app. Removing a track from a playlist does not delete the original music file.
  • You can remove a folder from the library. This does not erase every local reference; some information may be retained to support data recovery.
  • To remove data in the app’s container, use Delete App in iOS/iPadOS settings. Offload App may retain this data for reinstallation.
  • Files held by iCloud Drive or other providers, and any backups, are managed separately in those services. Removing the app is not a command to delete files stored by the provider.

The developer does not receive a copy of your library and cannot remotely delete data on your device.

05If you contact support

If you choose to send an email, its recipient receives your address and the content you send. This voluntary correspondence is separate from using the app: SONILY does not automatically send emails or attachments.

Send only what is needed. Avoid passwords, sensitive information or complete music files. Redact personal details from any screenshot you share.

Emails voluntarily sent to sonily.support@gmail.com are handled by Manuele Nobili through Gmail to respond to support and privacy requests. Gmail is a Google service: see Google’s Privacy Policy. Correspondence, including the sender’s address, messages and voluntary attachments, is handled separately from app data. SONILY does not collect information for the developer and does not automatically transmit app data by email.

Purpose and legal basis. Manuele Nobili processes the information you choose to provide to answer requests, provide support and handle follow-up correspondence. For ordinary support requests, the legal basis is the legitimate interest in providing effective support to SONILY users under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, only insofar as necessary and provided that your interests or fundamental rights do not override that interest. Voluntarily sending an email is not treated as general consent to other uses. Requests to exercise privacy rights are handled to comply with applicable legal obligations under Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

Email retention. Correspondence is retained for a maximum of 12 months from the last interaction. Longer retention is limited to legal obligations or the need to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, for as long as necessary for those purposes. The Gmail mailbox is managed in accordance with this policy.

06Privacy of this website

These pages use no analytics, advertising, data collection forms, profiling cookies or resources loaded from external services. They do not store preferences in your browser.

When the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. This hosting activity is separate from the SONILY app’s behaviour. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and GitHub Pages information.

IP logging also applies when the visitor is not signed into GitHub. GitHub’s statement also describes its processing of technical data, such as browser and device information, usage data and service logs, as well as recipients, international transfers and retention criteria. The 12-month email retention limit in this policy does not apply to GitHub logs. This provider processing does not mean that the SONILY app collects your library or listening data for the developer.

07Contact and updates

Where provided by the GDPR, you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of information supplied by email. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your particular situation; objections will be assessed under Article 21 GDPR. Write to sonily.support@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali) or the competent supervisory authority. There is no SONILY account to delete.
