01Start with your library

SONILY is an offline music player for iPhone and iPad, designed to play your files and organize them in folders, favorites and personal playlists. It does not provide a streaming music catalog.

  1. Open Library and choose Open music folder.
  2. Select a folder in the iOS/iPadOS Files picker and grant access.
  3. Tap a track to play it. Use its menu to add it to favorites or a playlist.

02Files, artwork and playback

Supported formats include MP3, FLAC, M4A, AAC and WAV. Compatibility also depends on each file’s encoding and content.

SONILY reads embedded metadata and artwork when available and decodable. If no usable cover is available, it displays the default SONILY artwork. For FLAC files with multiple images, AVFoundation may not expose every cover; recovery of every embedded image is not guaranteed.

Before listening offline to a file held by a cloud provider, make sure it is available on your device.

03Frequently asked questions

A folder or some tracks are no longer visible.

Check in Files that the folder is still available and the tracks are downloaded. If the folder moved or access is no longer valid, select it again in SONILY. Avoid deleting the app as a first troubleshooting step: you would lose local data not recovered from a backup.

How do I remove a track from a playlist?

Open the playlist and swipe sideways on the track row. This removes it from the playlist, not the original file or its favorites status.

Why are the Living Artwork bars not moving?

Living Artwork applies to the default SONILY artwork when a track has no usable cover. Check the switch in Appearance and make sure playback is active. Embedded covers are not converted into animated bars.

Music stopped after an audio device disconnected.

Disconnecting an audio output may pause playback. Check the selected audio output and resume manually with Play.

04Contact support

For support or privacy questions, contact Manuele Nobili, SONILY’s independent developer and privacy contact.

Support and privacy email

sonily.support@gmail.com

Voluntary emails are handled through Gmail, separately from app data. SONILY does not collect information for the developer and does not automatically send emails or attachments.

To help investigate a problem, include your device model, iOS/iPadOS version, SONILY version, file format and steps to reproduce it. Do not send passwords or sensitive information. Attach redacted screenshots only if needed.

Before writing, read the Privacy Policy section about support correspondence.
